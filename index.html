What Happened?

The Diwali Gift Selection email you interacted with was part of an internal cybersecurity awareness exercise. Attackers frequently use rewards, gifts, vouchers, bonuses and festive offers to persuade employees to click links and engage with fraudulent websites.

🚩 Red Flags in the Email

  • ⚠ Urgent response deadline
  • ⚠ Request to click a link
  • ⚠ Attractive reward/gift offer
  • ⚠ Unexpected action requested
  • ⚠ External web page interaction

✅ Security Best Practices

  • ✔ Verify the sender before acting
  • ✔ Inspect links before clicking
  • ✔ Be cautious of urgency tactics
  • ✔ Report suspicious emails
  • ✔ Contact IT/ISMS Team directly when unsure
